About KERC

The Kogi State Electricity Regulatory Commission (KERC) was established in 2024 pursuant to the Kogi State Electricity Law 2024, following the electricity sector reforms introduced by Nigeria's Electricity Act 2023. KERC serves as an independent regulatory body responsible for overseeing and regulating electricity operations within Kogi State.
