Shaping the Future of Electricity Regulation in Kogi State

We are dedicated to ensuring sustainable and reliable electricity to the served, underserved and unserved communities in Kogi State.

KERC's Vision

To be a leading regulatory authority driving universal access to sustainable, efficient and affordable electricity while empowering the people of Kogi State and fostering socio-economic growth through innovative and transparent governance in the power sector.

IGET Core Values

I - Integrity

Upholding ethics and professionalism in electricity market regulation


G - Good governance

Ensuring transparency and accountability in decision-making.


E - Efficiency

Ensuring timely and cost effective delivery of regulatory services for the benefit of stakeholders.


T - Team work

Fostering teamwork to achieve shared goals and enhance regulatory outcomes.

Enabling efficiency in power sector

Promoting transparency, fairness, and efficiency in the electricity sector.
