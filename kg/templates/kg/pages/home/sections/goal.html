The Kogi State Electricity Regulatory Commission (KERC) is an independent regulatory body with authority over the regulation of the electricity market in Kogi State. KERC was formed in 2024 under the Electricity Act of 2023 and the amended Act of 2024, which was duly signed by the President. This legislation requests every state to establish its own Electricity Law, empowering the state to participate in the electricity market by:

  • Ensuring the review of electricity tariffs
  • Implementing transparent policies regarding subsidies
  • Promoting policies that are efficient and environmentally friendly
  • Forming and enforcing standards in the creation and use of electricity within the state
